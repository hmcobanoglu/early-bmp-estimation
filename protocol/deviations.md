4. 2026-11-15 (after the locked evaluation, following an internal review): additional descriptive analyses were added and are labelled as such in the manuscript: (a) the between-laboratory mean absolute log deviation of the reference BMP, used as a comparator on the same scale as the prediction error because the mean absolute error and the standard deviation s_L are different quantities; (b) the paired laboratory-level comparisons prespecified in section 5 were computed (they had been omitted from the first results script); (c) errors grouped by the same-test cellulose yield on day 10 relative to the training mean (post hoc); (d) the source of the difference between the recalculated IL reference and the organisers' bottle values. The laboratory x material term in the s_L model (section 7) was not fitted; s_L is reported from the model with a laboratory random effect only.
5. 2026-11-15 (after two external pre-submission reviews): further descriptive checks were added without refitting any rule (`13_review_checks.py`, `14_manuscript_tables.py`): (a) recalculation of the IL organisers' net SMP from cumulative CH4 and blank bottles (agreement only when substrate VS is the organisers' fixed reference VS, c_vs_sub_f; the 1% net 3 d criterion is unaffected by the VS basis); (b) the cellulose-ratio grouping of deviation 4c recomputed with the training mean taken from the other laboratories only, test-level counts and the same grouping for the class-completion ratio; (c) reference at each bottle's own criterion day vs bottle mean at the condition completion day; (d) per-material and per-laboratory errors and leave-one-laboratory-out ranges in the IL primary set; (e) between-laboratory deviation and repeatability for IL round 1 accepted conditions; (f) paired differences from the class-completion ratio for all rules. The control yield is taken from the cellulose condition of the test with the lowest nominal ISR when a test contained more than one; the cellulose screen uses the ISR-2 condition.
